---
title: Vault
description: Keep private notes and files encrypted on your devices.
---

The vault encrypts notes and files on your device before they sync. The server, its administrator and its backups hold only their encrypted contents. Ordinary notes outside the vault remain readable by someone with server or backup access.

## Set up your vault

1. Connect to your server over HTTPS and sign in. Each account has its own vault.
2. Tap the header's lock button, **Open the vault**, or open **Settings > Vault** and choose **Set up**.
3. Choose a **Vault password** of at least eight characters and repeat it. This password is separate from your account password; use a different one.
4. Decide whether to **Remember on this device**, then select **Create vault**.
5. Save the recovery code somewhere safe outside the vault, then select **I have saved it**. Catch shows it only once.

<Callout type="warn" title="Keep the password or recovery code">
  Nobody can reset your vault password for you, including an administrator. If you lose both the password and recovery code, your vault notes cannot be recovered. Resetting your account password does not unlock the vault.
</Callout>

## Write and organize private notes

Enter the vault with **Open the vault** and unlock it when asked. Gallery, Deck, Search, Reminders, Archive and Trash then show only vault notes. Any new note you create there belongs to the vault. Use **Leave the vault** to return to ordinary notes.

You can edit, color, tag, pin and organize vault notes with the usual controls. Search runs on your device while the vault is unlocked. Vault notes sync as encrypted data and work offline once this device has synced them. Setting up, changing or recovering the password, and deleting the vault need a server connection.

Attachments, including their filenames, are encrypted too. The device makes encrypted thumbnails; a video downloads in full before playing. Prepare files you need with [Keep offline](/docs/using-catch/attachments#prepare-for-going-offline).

Reminders work, but notifications say **Vault note** instead of showing its text. Opening one enters the vault and asks for the password if locked. The server still knows reminder times, note sizes and change times, and attachment sizes.

The vault currently has these limits:

- No link previews or Catch sharing links.
- No moving ordinary notes into the vault. Their text could remain in older server backups even after a move.
- No importing into the vault or receiving shares from another app into it. Those notes go to ordinary Gallery.
- Changes from different devices replace the whole vault note when they sync. Let a note sync before editing it elsewhere; there is no conflict merge. Encrypted [version history](/docs/using-catch/version-history) lets you review and recover saved content while the vault is unlocked.

## Choose how the vault locks on this device

Each device decides how the vault locks and opens there. Find these choices in **Settings > Vault** under **This device**; they change only while the vault is unlocked.

**Lock automatically** is on unless you chose **Remember on this device** when setting up or unlocking. While it is on, the vault locks when you leave it or reload Catch, and also:

- after five minutes with Catch in the background, if it opens with its password;
- after one minute in the background, or five minutes on screen with no input, if it opens with a PIN or fingerprint.

Turn **Lock automatically** off to keep the vault open here, including after a restart. Anyone who can open Catch on this device can then open the vault, so do this only on a device you trust. It also turns off this device's PIN and fingerprint unlock.

**Lock now** locks the vault and forgets a remembered key, but keeps the PIN and fingerprint unlock. Signing out forgets all three.

### Unlock with a PIN

With **Lock automatically** on, turn on **Unlock with a PIN** and choose four to eight digits. The vault then opens here with the PIN instead of its password. The PIN belongs to this device only; set one on each device where you want it. Use **Change** beside **Change PIN** to replace it.

After five wrong PINs in a row, the device forgets the PIN and asks for the vault password. Set a PIN again once you are in.

### Unlock with a fingerprint on Android

In the Android app, on a phone with a fingerprint or other strong biometric set up, turn on **Unlock with fingerprint** (some phones call it **Unlock with biometrics**). Entering the vault then asks for your fingerprint first. **Use PIN** or **Use password** on the prompt falls back to the PIN, if you set one, or the password. Catch does not use the phone's screen-lock PIN.

If the fingerprints enrolled on the phone change, fingerprint unlock turns itself off. After you next unlock with the PIN or password, Catch offers to turn it back on.

### Hide the vault button

**Settings > Vault > Show vault button** hides or shows the header shortcut on this device, across its accounts. It does not delete or disable the vault; you can still open it from Settings or a reminder. The leave button stays visible while you are inside.

## Change or recover the password

In **Settings > Vault**, choose **Change** beside **Vault password**. Enter the current vault password and repeat the new one. The recovery code stays the same, and devices that remember the vault keep it open.

If you have forgotten the password, select **Use the recovery code** on the unlock screen. Enter the saved code and choose a new vault password. This also keeps the same recovery code.

## Delete the vault

In **Settings > Vault**, choose **Delete** beside **Delete vault**, then confirm **Delete vault**. This removes every vault note and its files from the server and your synced devices. It does not need the vault password and cannot be undone. Older server backups remain separate encrypted copies; deleting the live vault does not erase them.
