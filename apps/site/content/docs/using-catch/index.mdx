title: User guide
description: Start here if you want to write and organize notes.
---

You need the address of a Catch server and an account on it. Ask the person running your server for the address or an invite link. Catch does not provide a hosted account service.

1. Follow [Installation and sign-in](/docs/using-catch/getting-started).
2. [Write your first note](/docs/using-catch/notes).
3. Use the [Gallery, Archive and Trash](/docs/using-catch/organizing), or put active work in the [Deck](/docs/using-catch/deck).
4. Add [tags](/docs/using-catch/tags), [attachments](/docs/using-catch/attachments), [links](/docs/using-catch/links-and-sharing) and [reminders](/docs/using-catch/reminders).
5. Learn how [offline changes and app updates](/docs/using-catch/offline-and-updates) work before relying on Catch away from a connection.

The bar at the bottom is the **dock**. It switches between **Deck**, **Gallery** and **Search**. When a note is open, the dock shows that note's controls instead. On a phone, a note opens over the page; on a wide screen, it opens in a pane beside it.

While on Gallery, tap **Gallery** in the dock again to choose **Reminders**, **Archive** or **Trash**. You can also hold the Gallery tab to open those choices. Open **Settings** with the settings button in the Gallery header.

[Settings and accounts](/docs/using-catch/settings-and-accounts) lists your preferences. [FAQ](/docs/using-catch/faq) covers privacy, exports and current limits. Administrative controls are covered separately in [Admin guide](/docs/running-a-server).
