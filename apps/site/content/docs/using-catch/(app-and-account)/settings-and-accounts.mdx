Open **Settings** from the Gallery header. On a wide screen, pages are listed beside the current one. On a phone, the dock's settings picker switches pages.

## General

- **Appearance**: choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **App icon**: when your server runs a preview release, choose the stable or preview icon for this device. An installed web app may ask you to approve the change.
- **Time format**: choose **Automatic**, **12 hour** or **24 hour**. Automatic follows the browser's language convention; in the native Android app it follows the phone's clock setting.
- **Notes > Link previews**: show or hide preview cards. Your server must also allow fetching previews.
- **Link capture**: install the browser [bookmarklet](/docs/using-catch/links-and-sharing#add-the-browser-bookmarklet).

Appearance, app icon and time format are preferences on this device. Gallery layout, sorting, collapsed columns, recent searches and the archived-search preference are also local view choices.

## Tags, Notifications and Data Management

**Tags** manages your [tag tree](/docs/using-catch/tags). **Notifications** controls [reminder delivery, snooze and quick times](/docs/using-catch/reminders). Delivery is enabled per device; snooze and quick times belong to your account across devices.

**Data Management** currently contains the [Google Keep importer](/docs/using-catch/google-keep). It does not offer a general note export or a personal backup/restore tool. Administrators have whole-server backups under Admin.

**Update** shows versions, releases and [update actions](/docs/using-catch/offline-and-updates).

## Vault

**Vault** sets up and manages your encrypted [vault](/docs/using-catch/vault). You can unlock or lock it, choose how it locks and opens on this device (automatically, with a PIN or, on Android, with a fingerprint), hide the header shortcut, change its password or delete it. Its password is separate from your account password; an account password reset cannot open the vault.

## Change your password

Open **Account**, then **Change password**. Enter your current and new passwords and confirm the change. The new password must have 8–128 characters. Select **Save password** to apply it. Changing it signs out your other sessions; they need to sign in again to sync.

If you forget it, ask an administrator to reset it. Catch has no email password-recovery flow. The administrator gives you a temporary password; sign in and replace it in Account settings. Temporary passwords do not automatically expire.

**Account** also shows who is signed in and, on Android, the server's address. There is no server-switching control here; the Android setup connects the app to one server. Different Android channels have independent app data.

## Use several accounts

Tap your avatar in the Gallery header to open the account list. Select **Add account** to sign in to another account on the same server without signing the first one out. Select an account to switch to it; a swipe across the avatar also switches accounts.

Drag an account by its handle to reorder the list, or focus the handle and use the arrow keys. The order is also the order a swipe across the avatar follows.

Close any open note or composer first. Switching reloads the app, keeps each account's local data separate and does not delete its queued work. That work sends when the account is open again. Browser tabs can show different accounts at the same time.

When several accounts are signed in, browser addresses contain the account's place in that list: the first is `/u/1`, the next `/u/2`, as in `/u/2/archive`. Reordering the accounts changes their numbers; open tabs stay on their account and move to its new number. A bookmark uses the number, so after a reorder it may open a different account. The number belongs to this device; the same number need not identify the same account on another device. A Catch note URL is not a way to share access.

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** in Account settings, or the sign-out control beside an account in the avatar list. Catch removes only that account from this device. Its server notes remain, but its local database, pending changes and files are deleted. With another account signed in, Catch switches to it.

Wait for pending work to sync first. The warning about unsynced changes is a warning that they will be deleted, not an offer to save them elsewhere.

Administrators also see **Admin > Users** and **Admin > Backups**. Those controls require a connection and are covered in the [admin guide](/docs/running-a-server).
