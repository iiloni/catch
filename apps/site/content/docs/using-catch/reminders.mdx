---
title: Set reminders
description: Choose times, repeats and notifications on each device.
---

## Set a reminder on a note

1. Open the note and select **Reminder** in the dock.
2. Choose **Today**, **Tomorrow** or a **Custom** day.
3. Choose **Morning**, **Afternoon**, **Evening** or a **Custom** time.
4. Adjust **Repeat** and **Time zone** if needed, then select **Save**.

A note has one reminder. Open the bell or reminder chip again to edit it; **Remove reminder** deletes it. Setting a reminder does not change the note's last-edited time. Trashed notes do not ring.

Use the Gallery dock tab's choices to open **Reminders**, which separates upcoming reminders from **Past** ones. A recurring reminder moves to its next occurrence when it rings, without waiting for you to acknowledge it.

## Repeat and choose a time zone

**Repeat** offers daily, weekly, monthly and yearly schedules with an interval. Weekly repeats can use selected weekdays. Monthly repeats can use a date or a weekday such as the last Monday. You can end a repeat on a date or after a number of occurrences.

A monthly date beyond the end of a shorter month falls on that month's last day. Times skipped by a clock change move forward by that change; a time that occurs twice rings once, at the first occurrence. Missed repeat occurrences are skipped rather than all delivered in a burst.

**Follow** uses your local clock as you travel: 9:00 stays 9:00 where you are. **Custom** fixes the reminder to a selected time zone, for example 9:00 in London wherever you are.

**Add to Google Calendar** opens Google's new-event form with the note's first text line and a link back to Catch. It does not copy the reminder's date, time or repeat, and there is no ongoing calendar sync. You finish and save the event in Google Calendar yourself.

## Turn on notifications

On each device, open **Settings > Notifications**, enable **Reminder notifications** and allow the permission request. Use **Test > Send** to check delivery. If blocked, allow notifications in the browser's site settings or Android's app settings, then enable them in Catch again.

On iPhone or iPad, open Catch from its Home Screen icon before enabling notifications. If the browser cannot receive them, Settings explains that limitation.

Browser notifications are sent by your server through your browser's push service, encrypted to your device. They need the server and its internet connection. A browser cannot ring Catch reminders locally while the server is unavailable.

The native Android app rings reminders locally, including offline and while closed, once it has received them. Reminders made on another device need to reach the phone first. A background check tries to refresh them, but Android controls when it runs; battery saving can delay it. Open Catch online to make sure the phone has the latest reminders.

## Snooze and adjust quick times

Snooze from a reminder's controls in Catch. Native Android notifications also offer **Snooze** and **Done**. Done dismisses the notification; it does not delete the reminder or complete its note.

In **Settings > Notifications**, choose a snooze length of 15 minutes, 30 minutes (the default) or one hour. Set **Morning**, **Afternoon** and **Evening** under **Quick times**. These preferences apply across your devices; changing a quick time does not rewrite existing reminders.

An Android notification snooze reaches the server and other devices when you next use that account in Catch. Each enabled device rings separately. Notifications apply to all accounts signed in on a device, not only the one currently open, and dismissing on one device does not dismiss on the others.
