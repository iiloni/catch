---
title: Gallery
description: Pin, sort, select, archive and delete notes.
---

## Keep notes in the Gallery

**Gallery** holds notes that are neither in the Deck, archived nor trashed. Pin a note with **Pin** to put it in **Pinned**, above **Others**. **Unpin** returns it to the ordinary group.

Open **Sort** to choose **Custom**, **Last edited** or **Date created**. Date sorts offer **Newest first** and **Oldest first**. **Custom** lets you drag notes into your own order. On touch, hold a card before dragging it; releasing the hold without dragging selects it instead. Pinned and unpinned groups remain separate.

At narrow widths, **Switch to single column view** and **Switch to masonry view** change how cards are arranged. This layout choice is remembered on the device. Wider galleries use the masonry layout.

## Select several notes

Hold a note to select it, or use its selection checkbox with a mouse. Tap other notes to add or remove them. While selecting, tapping a card changes the selection instead of opening it.

Actions appear in the page header. Depending on the page, you can **Pin**, change **Background color**, apply **Tags**, **Archive**, **Move to trash**, **Make a copy**, **Restore**, **Delete forever**, or **Send to gallery** from the Deck. Close the selection or press back or Escape to return to normal browsing.

## Archive notes you want to keep

Choose **Archive** on an open note or a selected group. A Gallery card can also be swiped sideways to archive it. Archive keeps the note and its files but removes it from the Gallery or Deck.

To find it, return to Gallery and tap the **Gallery** dock tab again, then select **Archive**. Open the note and choose **Unarchive**, or unarchive selected notes. A note returns to its Gallery or Deck destination. Search includes archived notes by default.

## Move notes to Trash

Choose **Move to trash**. Catch offers **Undo** after trashing. To find trashed notes, use the Gallery dock tab's page choices and select **Trash**.

Trashed notes keep their attachments. They are read-only until you choose **Restore**. They are excluded from search. Catch does not automatically empty Trash after a fixed number of days.

**Delete forever** permanently removes a note and its attachments. **Empty trash** removes every note currently in Trash after confirmation. Ordinary editing undo cannot recover a permanently deleted note; an administrator's server restore affects the whole server, not just that note.
